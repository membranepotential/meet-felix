# Google Workspace cost baseline for Meet transcripts (DE, EUR)

Checked 2026-10-09. Labels: [V] = read on an official Google page (via WebFetch, which summarises the page, so wording is second-hand); [U] = unverified (third-party or not found).

## Takeaway
- Cheapest official plan with plain Meet transcripts: Business Standard. Starter has no recording to Drive and is not on the transcript list. [V]
- Business Standard list price on workspace.google.com/intl/de/pricing: 13,60 EUR/user/month, excl. VAT, shown under the "annual (16 % saving)" option. With 19 % German VAT that is about 16,18 EUR. [V] for 13,60 net; the VAT arithmetic is mine.
- So "about 17 EUR/month" is plausible for Business Standard, but I cannot prove the exact figure. The page extract showed no separate flexible price. If annual = 16 % off flexible, flexible would be about 16,19 net / 19,27 gross (derived, [U]). Felix should check his invoice: net or gross, annual or flexible, and which plan name.
- Likely he pays for Business Standard (the Gemini/Meet bundle). He cannot get transcripts cheaper from Workspace; only a consumer path may be cheaper (see Q4, mostly [U] for price).

## 2. Meet features by plan
From pricing page [V]: participants Starter 100 / Standard 150 / Plus 500 / Enterprise 1000; max meeting length 24 h on all four; recording to Drive: not on Starter, yes on Standard/Plus/Enterprise; Gemini notes: not on Starter.
Transcripts, https://support.google.com/meet/answer/12849897 [V]: Business Standard, Business Plus, Enterprise Starter/Standard/Plus, Teaching and Learning Upgrade, Education Plus, Workspace Individual. Computer or Android only. Languages: English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Spanish. Saved to organiser's Drive "Google Meet" folder. Not on Business Starter.
Take notes for me (Gemini notes): needs an eligible Workspace edition or Google AI plan; the organiser must have it (https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14754931) [V]. The exact edition list was not on that page; the pricing page shows Gemini notes for Standard and up [V].
Translated captions: Business Standard, Business Plus, Enterprise tiers, Google AI Pro for Education (https://support.google.com/meet/answer/9300310) [V].
Live captions availability by plan: page not retrieved [U] (commonly free, not confirmed).

## 4. Google AI Pro / One
- Blog 2026-06-29 https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/workspace/take-notes-for-me/ : "Take notes for me" now available to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers (and eligible Workspace business customers), web and mobile, select languages, only for meetings you organise, participants are notified [V].
- It transcribes and writes notes to a Google Doc; it is not the same as the separate Transcripts/Record features [search summary, U].
- EUR prices: https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/?hl=de showed "4,99 / 9,99 EUR" for AI Plus (unclear which is which); AI Pro and Ultra prices were missing from the extract. AI Pro EUR price: [U]. AI Plus Meet notes: not stated.

## 6. Is 17 EUR = Business Standard flexible?
Not provably. Standard annual 13,60 net = 16,18 gross; derived flexible ~16,19 net = 19,27 gross. 17 EUR fits neither exactly. Possible: rounded, discount, different plan, or older price. Ask Felix for the invoice line.

## Baseline for the self-built alternative
~13,60 EUR net (~16,20 gross) per month, or ~16,2 net on flexible (derived).
Cheaper consumer option (AI Pro) only if its price is below that and 1:1 hosting of own meetings suffices: price [U].
